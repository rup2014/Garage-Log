# Privacy Policy

Garage Log doesn't collect, see or store your garage. It's saved in your own browser and, if you turn on sync, in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking cookies. Optional features (Google Drive sync, AI part design with your own Anthropic API key) talk to Google and Anthropic directly from your browser.

Read the full policy at **https://rup2014.github.io/Garage-Log/privacy.html** (source: [`privacy.html`](privacy.html)).
