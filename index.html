      Settings

      Sync across devices

      Keep your garage in step on your phone and computer. It's saved in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive; Garage Log can't see anything else in your Drive, and no one else gets a copy.

      Back up your garage

      Download a copy of your garage to keep it safe or move it somewhere without sync. Uploaded 3D models aren't included in the file; Google Drive sync does include them.

      AI part design (optional)

      To have Claude design 3D parts the library doesn't recognize, paste an Anthropic API key from console.anthropic.com. It's stored only in this browser and sent only to Anthropic, together with the part's name, brand and notes and your car's make, model and size. Each design costs a few cents on your Anthropic account, and your use is covered by Anthropic's terms. AI designs are rough visual approximations, not real part specifications.
